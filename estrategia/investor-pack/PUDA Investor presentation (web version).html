The trading strategy

Eleven automated agents that never put at risk the reserve backing PUDA.

Different strategies that do not depend on the same market direction: neutral, hedged long and a short account that profits in falls. What they can lose in a year is capped at the reserve's yield.

PUDA · Investor presentation

08

PUDA's backing

The reserve is never put at risk

Backing reserve

100% in custody, off the exchange: stablecoins in regulated custody, Treasury bills and gold.

→

Annual yield

≈ 4% of the reserve*. It is the only money the system can lose in a year.

→

Trading system

If the year's losses reach the yield, all eleven agents stop until the next year.

In the worst year, the reserve ends as it started: at 100%.

* Indicative: depends on interest rates. PUDA's market price can fall; the backing of each token cannot.

11

Risk management

Five layers of protection

Every order

Mandatory stop loss: the system rejects an order without one or with one more than 25% away.

Every position

Isolated margin: a position can only lose its own margin.

Every subaccount

At most 20% at play; 14-day pause at −10% and permanent stop at −20%.

The whole system

Maximum annual loss = the reserve's yield. Once reached, everything stops until the next year.

The reserve

Off the exchange and in custody. Never used for trading.
