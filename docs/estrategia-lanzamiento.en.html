The PUDA launch strategy, improved

A review of the strategy peludacoin.com publishes today (tokenomics, presale, roadmap, bot and compliance) and an improved version. It rests on three things: El Salvador's regulation (LEAD, the digital assets issuance law, and the CNAD, the National Digital Assets Commission), the bot's 3-year stress test, and whether the numbers add up.

This is not legal or financial advice. The proposals are a starting point to work through with a Salvadoran lawyer and a Spanish one. Example figures are marked as such.

2 · Principles

Five rules for the new strategy

  1. Register first, sell later. Not a single dollar is accepted until the CNAD has registered the offering, and the sale runs through a PSAD (licensed digital asset service provider) with KYC.
  2. No promised returns. Only real, audited bot results are published, together with their drawdown, never targets such as “25% a year”.
  3. Everything verifiable on-chain. An audited sale contract, a multisig treasury, liquidity locked by contract and vesting built into the token itself.
  4. The bot proves itself before it is funded. Six months on demo with public read-only results, and only the strategies that passed the stress test.
  5. Offer only where it is legal. El Salvador and the jurisdictions that allow it. Geo-block the EU and the US until MiCA (the EU crypto-asset regulation) and securities rules are met.
3 · Tokenomics

Consistent numbers and less dilution

The current model adds up to 105% (15% initial + 85% emissions + 5% team). On top of that, issuing and selling every year at market price heavily dilutes the first buyers. Move the controls to see the effect. The starting values are the website’s.

What changes compared with the website

  • The team comes out of the total; it is no longer added on top.
  • The circulating market cap (circulating × price) is published, and separately the fully diluted valuation. Never 617.4 M as the “Year 1 market cap”.
  • Future emissions stop being an automatic sale: each tranche is registered, capped and paired with buybacks funded by real profits.
  • An initial price of 10 USD with 61.74 M tokens implies a 617 M valuation. A lower price or a smaller initial sale makes the valuation defensible to the certifier.
4 · The bot

What can be said about the bot, according to the stress test

The 3-year test ran the strategies’ real code through synthetic crash, bear-market and sideways scenarios. It measures risk, not returns, but that is enough to decide what must not be promised.

  • Track record before money: 6 months on Bitget Demo, then with small own capital. Public results through a read-only API key and a monthly NAV report.
  • Bot capital capped at a share of the funds raised, with a hard stop at 40% drawdown and kept separate from the treasury.
  • Platform risk: Bitget did not hold a MiCA licence as of mid-September 2026. The Salvadoran entity must confirm which exchanges it may trade on and have a plan B.
  • No “AI” as a hook: describe the concrete rules and risk limits. The website’s “AI with backup mirror” adds nothing verifiable.
