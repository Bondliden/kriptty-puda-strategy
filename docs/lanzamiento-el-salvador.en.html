PeludaCoin (PUDA) · roadmap · October 2026

Creating and launching PUDA in El Salvador

Steps to issue the token under the Ley de Emisión de Activos Digitales (LEAD, Digital Assets Issuance Law) and the Comisión Nacional de Activos Digitales (CNAD, National Digital Assets Commission). The roadmap starts with a single professional in the pre-launch phase, the auditor, and explains what else the law requires before any sale.

⚖️

This is not legal advice. It is based on public sources consulted on 1 October 2026, some of which are secondary summaries. Each step states whether it has been verified. Steps marked lawyer require a Salvadoran lawyer; EU matters require a Spanish one.

The short answer

Can we start with just an auditor?

Yes, during pre-launch, as long as the token is neither sold nor promoted. For the public offering the LEAD requires further roles, and none of them is simply an “auditor”.

Phase 0 · now

Smart contract auditor

Reviews the ERC-20 contract, the vesting and the per-wallet limits. The law does not require it as a separate role, but its report feeds the technical section of the offering document. It is what the website already promises with “Audit First”.

Phase 2 · before selling

Certificador (certifier) registered with the CNAD

This is the role the LEAD does require for a public offering: it analyses the financial, legal and technical information in the offering document and reports to the CNAD. It must be independent and cannot also act as the structurer.

Phase 1–2

Estructurador (structurer) + local lawyer

The structurer drafts the Documento de Información Relevante (DIR, offering document). In addition, the issuer must be domiciled in El Salvador or use a platform based there, and the sale is made through licensed digital asset service providers (PSAD).

Before hiring anyone

Fix what the website says today

The LEAD prohibits promoting an offering before it is registered. In addition, several statements on PeludaRemake describe audited returns and guarantees that do not yet exist. A certifier or a regulator will read them as an investment offer.

    Spain and the EU follow their own law

    MiCA (EU Regulation 2023/1114)

    • Registering the token in El Salvador does not allow it to be offered to the public in Spain.
    • To offer it in the EU, the offeror (a legal person) notifies the white paper to the CNMV 20 working days before publishing it. There is no prior approval, but marketing rules apply.
    • Since 23 December 2025 the CNMV only accepts white papers through its “CWP” electronic procedure, in iXBRL format using the ESMA template.

    Main risk

    • If PUDA holders earn from the profits of a trading bot, the token may be a financial instrument. In that case it would fall outside MiCA and within securities regulation (prospectus and MiFID; in Spain, Law 6/2023). unverified for PUDA
    • Managing the issuer from Spain may create Spanish tax residence through its “place of effective management” and cancel out the Salvadoran tax advantages. general principle
    • Bitget did not hold a MiCA licence as of mid-September 2026: bear this in mind if you plan to list the token or run the bot from Spain.
