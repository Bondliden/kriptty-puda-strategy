A · Company Step 1 — Prior decisions

What must be decided before going to the notary

  • Who issues: a company domiciled in El Salvador (S.A. de C.V., a Salvadoran corporation). The LEAD (Digital Asset Issuance Law) requires a Salvadoran issuer or a platform domiciled there.
  • Partners and capital: who the shareholders are, how much each contributes, and how the team's 3.09 M PUDA are split (5%, with a 1-year cliff and 4-year vesting).
  • Where it is run from: if the company is managed from Spain it may become Spanish tax resident through its "place of effective management" and lose the LEAD benefits.
  • One or two companies: separating the token issuer from the bot operations reduces risk and keeps the accounts clear.
First of all: a Salvadoran lawyer (structure and LEAD) and a Spanish tax adviser (residence and partners' taxation). They are the first hires, together with the auditor.

      C · Bot What the test found

      Six flaws that would have cost real money

      Hidden leverage

      SUB2 and SUB11 reached 9× capital. The cap now counts open positions.

      Backtester without margin

      It allowed positions the exchange would reject. It now requires margin.

      SUB6 in a loop

      It opened and unwound pairs, paying fees each time. Fixed.

      SUB7 and SUB4

      They lost in every scenario (−95% and −52% in a month). Disabled.

      Chained pauses

      25% drawdowns one after another. New hard stop at 40%.

      Unlimited margin

      New limit of 20% of capital per account, applied to every order.

        D · Returns18
        Risks and conditions

        What could stop the plan

        MiCA and Bitget

        Bitget had no MiCA license as of September 2026, and the CNMV (Spanish securities regulator) treats perpetuals as CFDs for retail clients: confirm where trading is done from and through which entity.

        Token classification

        If holders profit from the bot, it may be a security: outside MiCA in the EU and subject to prospectus requirements.

        Unproven returns

        Without real history or demo, any figure is a hypothesis. At 7x, a mistake costs three times as much as at 2x.

        Tax residence

        Managing the company from Spain may void the Salvadoran tax regime.

        Platform risk

        Reliance on a single exchange: a plan B and balance limits per platform.

        Execution

        The timeline depends on third parties: auditor, certifier, CNAD and PSAD.
